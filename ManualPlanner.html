Before you use this in Angel One

  • Use your broker's live bid/ask; confirm instrument, exchange, product eligibility, lot/tick size, holdings and available cash.
  • Intraday: independently confirm today's session, broker square-off deadline and exit feasibility. This page does not monitor or close positions.
  • Delivery: long only, funded cash; DP estimate assumes settled holdings. No unborrowed overnight short or margin funding.
  • One entry plus one complete exit only. Multiple entries, scale-outs, partial executions across orders and DP grouping need separate charges.
  • Historical context is not a validated trading strategy. No future profit, full-history verification or calibrated edge is claimed.
